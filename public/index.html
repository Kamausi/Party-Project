Make your move.

Use an item, roll 1–10, and choose your route at intersections. Blue spaces give currency. Red spaces take it.

02

Chase the objective.

Save 20 currency to buy the marked objective as you pass it. Once claimed, it moves. So should your plans.

03

Settle it in a minigame.

After everyone takes a turn, compete in a quick challenge. Win currency, fund your next move, and take the lead.

Be a good rival.

Each player has a numbered marker as well as a color. Local players share board controls in turn, then use their own keys or touch buttons in minigames. CPU players fill the remaining seats.
